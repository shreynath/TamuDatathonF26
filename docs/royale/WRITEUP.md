# Gilded Ace

The manager wants to replace the 10 machines with the lowest house win from January through June 2025. Raw win is a bad sort. Rank 1 means the lowest value, not the best. M-1026, which looks weak at raw rank 42 of 310, is rank 265 on residual per available hour: 264 machines did worse against theo after hours. It is not a replacement candidate.

Of the actual bottom 10, one machine fails a luck test. M-1158 won $15,796 against $36,165 less theo than expected. Its residual-per-hour rank is 2, and its Holm-adjusted p-value is 0.0026. The other nine are not significant after Holm correction across the floor. That test is a normal approximation to daily residuals, not proof of a fault.

Downtime is small next to the floor. Modeled lost theo, using scheduled hours minus available hours times the observed theo rate, is $90,313 over six months. Ten percent of that is $9,031, below the $11,520 technician shift, so the shift is not funded. Thirty and fifty percent would clear it; those cases are not the plan.

Carded guests are a different total from the meters. 4,382 of 113,890 carded players produce half of carded coin-in. Comp cost per carded coin-in moves from 0.105% in January to 0.124% in June. That drift is too small to buy a floor-wide comp change.

The forecast uses January–April weekday means against a constant mean, then May–June. Weekday MAE is $68,699. Constant MAE is $218,258. Staff from the weekday pattern. It does not pick a machine.

Spend $1,200 to inspect M-1158. Keep $98,800. Do not buy a replacement until the next 30 gaming days still show the gap.
