# Dashboard Royale questions

Transcribed from the cloned GUIDE.md section "The challenge in 2 minutes."

1. Which machines are truly underperforming once you account for exposure, availability and luck?
2. What changed in demand across the floor over the six months, and why?
3. How much play was lost to downtime, and when?
4. What did tracked players wager, get back and net, and how concentrated are those results?
5. What did the loyalty (comp) program cost, and did anything about it change?
6. Which actions would you fund with $100,000, and how would you know they worked?

Question 1 is the case in `apps/web/public/ace_case.json`. Questions 2 through 5 are only partly covered by the forecast and the tracked-player totals in that file. They are not marked complete.
